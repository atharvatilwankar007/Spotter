# Ghost Set shot list

Two jobs from one gym session: clips to tune the form checks, and footage for the motion-graphic film.

## How to film

- Use the phone's normal **camera app**, not Ghost Set. Ghost Set never saves video, and I need the files.
- Portrait, 1080p, 30 fps. Phone on a tripod or wedged steady; nothing in front of you.
- Put the phone exactly where the Ghost Set setup screen says (side-on or facing) for that exercise.
- Each clip: walk in, **2 seconds still**, the set, 2 seconds still, walk out. One set per clip.
- Name clips like `pulldown-good.mp4`, `pulldown-swing.mp4`, and drop them all in `D:\Vibecoding\Spotter\clips\`.
- Light weight for the "bad" sets. Exaggerate the mistake a little but stay safe; skip any you're not comfortable faking.
- If a friend is there: have them film you from a second angle too. That's film B-roll, not needed for tuning.

## The list (≈ 20 clips, 6–8 reps each)

| # | Exercise | Phone | Clean set | Bad sets (one clip each) |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Lat pulldown | Side-on, seat height | ✅ bar to upper chest, torso still | Swing back to pull · Pull down to stomach · Behind the neck (light, careful) |
| 2 | Barbell or dumbbell curl | Side-on, hip height | ✅ elbows pinned | Elbows drift forward · Swing your body |
| 3 | Triceps pushdown | Side-on, hip height | ✅ upper arms locked | Upper arms moving · Rocking into it |
| 4 | Dumbbell row | Side-on, bench height | ✅ chest still | Jerking the torso up |
| 5 | Squat (or goblet squat) | Side-on, hip height | ✅ hips to knee level | Half squats · Leaning far forward |
| 6 | RDL | Side-on, hip height | ✅ bar brushing legs | Bar drifting away · Bending knees a lot · Rounding upper back (light!) |
| 7 | Overhead press | Facing the phone, chest height | ✅ full lockout | Stop short of lockout · One arm lagging |
| 8 | Lateral raise | Facing the phone, chest height | ✅ to shoulder height | Raising too high · One arm lagging |
| 9 | Push-up | Side-on, floor level | ✅ straight plank | Hips sagging |
| 10 | Wrong-exercise test | Side-on | — | Curl with "Squat" picked · Squat with "Curl" picked |

## For the film

- Do 2–3 takes of your favourite pair, e.g. **pulldown swinging → pulldown clean**: that's the story (app catches the mistake, you fix it, score goes up).
- Screen-record the phone (pull down the quick settings → Screen record) while Ghost Set runs on that pair, so we have the real app reacting.
- A few slow, well-lit close-ups: hands on the bar, the phone on its stand, the rest-timer alarm ringing.
